# Genesis Avatar Holders Rewards

Every Genesis Avatar Holder will get a FREE [VIP Pass](../purchases/vip-pass.md) for each week during the whole Season 4.

Make sure you have your Genesis Avatar(s) on AGW (Abstract Global Wallet). You can claim VIP Pass every week at ["Genesis Avatars" section. ](https://hub.tollan.io/#/genesis)

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Important:** A snapshot of Genesis Avatar Holders will be taken every Friday at 9:30PM UTC. Only wallets holding Genesis Avatars before the snapshot are eligible for VIP Pass.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="success" %}
You can get [Tollan Universe Genesis Avatars on OpenSea](https://opensea.io/collection/tollan-universe-genesis-avatars)
{% endhint %}

If you still have your Genesis Avatar(s) on zkIMX, make sure to migrate it to Abstract.&#x20;

**How to migrate:**

1. Go to [https://bridge.tollan.io/](https://bridge.tollan.io/)
2. Connect your MetaMask holding Genesis Avatar(s)
3. Click Migrate to Abstract (⚠️ Make sure you have IMX on Immutable and ETH on Abstract for gas fees)
4. Connect your AGW (Abstract Global Wallet)
5. Claim your Genesis Avatar on Abstract

Note: You will receive your Genesis Avatar in your MetaMask on Abstract chain. After that, you’ll need to manually transfer it to your AGW. It needs to be the same AGW you use for playing Tollan Universe to be eligible for VIP Pass during Season 4.

The official contract address: 0x3a85ba3b4fdcfbcf45340371408df9fd8c140ec1<br>
