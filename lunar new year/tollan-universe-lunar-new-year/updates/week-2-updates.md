# Week 2 Updates

#### **VIP Pass & Hunter’s Badge**

* The [VIP Pass](../purchases/vip-pass.md) and [Hunter’s Badge](../purchases/hunters-badge.md) are now active for **7 event days** from the moment of purchase, regardless of leaderboard resets.

#### **✳️** [**Abstract Stamps**](../overview/abstract-stamps.md) **Updates**

* The daily cap has been replaced with a **weekly cap**:
  * **Non-VIP:** up to **25,000** Stamps per week
  * **VIP:** up to **100,000** Stamps per week
* **Doubled Abstract Stamps rewards** for Top 100 players on both the Powerboard and Peak Run leaderboards.
* Using a 200× multiplier now gives you **10x of Abstract Stamps** earned during that run.

#### **Earn Abstract Stamps for FREE**

* Defeat Bonus Enemies in Practice Mode to earn 10–100 Abstract Stamps (25,000 weekly cap). 

#### Other Updates

* **Green dots** indicate the direction to the **closest keys** on the minimap
* Various visual improvements
* Bug fixes
* Balance updates
