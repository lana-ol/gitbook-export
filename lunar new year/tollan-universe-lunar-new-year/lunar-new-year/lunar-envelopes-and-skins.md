#### Lunar Envelopes

* Lunar Envelopes have 5% chance to drop from [Bonus Enemies](../gameplay/bonus-enemy.md) (subject to change)
* Only x1 Lunar Envelope can drop per run
* It can be opened within 24 hours, then it will expire&#x20;
* Prizes include Crowns, Powerboard Points, and Lunar Armor (see chance table for prizes below)
{% endcolumn %}

{% hint style="danger" %}

* Envelopes cannot be opened between Weeks
* Rewards are matched to the week during which they are opened
{% endhint %}

#### Lunar Skins Mint

* Lunar skin Mint will begin during Week 11 of Season 4. The exact date will be announced soon. All Lunar skins acquired after that will have an option to be immediately minted. Before the mint, all Lunar Skins will remain off-chain items in your inventory.
