# 🥟 Lunar Food

Crates, Vases, and Buildings have a small chance to drop Dumplings, Noodles, or Fortune Cookies. These apply the following effects when picked up:&#x20;



* Dumplings: Increase XP gain by 15% for 15 seconds.
* Noodles: Permanently increase Max HP by 2% and gain 0.01% Max HP Regen per second.
* Fortune Cookies: Randomly gain permanent 1% Damage, Movement Speed, or Cooldown Reduction.&#x20;
