# Elemental Affinity System

Every wave in this season comes with one or more **Elemental Affinities**: _**Fire**, **Water**, **Wind**, and **Energy**._

Enemies spawn with elemental shields matching the Wave's Affinities. To deal damage, you must first break the shield using abilities of the correct Elemental Affinity.

<div align="center" data-full-width="false"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/affinities-ezgif.com-cut (1).gif" alt="" width="191"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

Mastering your elemental setup becomes essential for getting through further waves.

#### Player Affinities

Each skill you learn belongs to an Elemental Affinity. Your Affinity Level for each Element equals the highest-level skill of that Affinity, up to a maximum of Level 6.&#x20;

_Example:_ If you have three Fire skills\
(Level 3, Level 5, and Level 6), Your Fire Affinity Level is 6.

During a run, only one skill can be upgraded to Level 6 (Evolution); all other skills are capped at Level 5, before you claim all [Shrines](shrines-and-keys-system.md).&#x20;

You can upgrade one additional skill  to Level 6 (Evolution) after all shrines have been claimed.
