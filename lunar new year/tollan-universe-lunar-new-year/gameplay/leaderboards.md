# Leaderboards

All leaderboards reset _every Friday at 3PM UTC_

{% hint style="success" %}
**100% of ETH Prizepool goes to Powerboard rewards**
{% endhint %}

Compete across **two Leaderboards** to win ETH and Abstract Stamps ✳️:

* **Powerboard:** Your cumulative score across all games, including the multipliers used. Top-100 earn a share of the ETH prizepool and Abstract Stamps. Rank 101–200 receive 2000 Abstract Stamps each.
* **Peak Run:** Your skill-based score, multiplier is not taken into account. Top-200 earn Abstract Stamps.&#x20;

#### Final Score Formula

* **Powerboard:** ((Base Score + Bonus Enemy Points + Shrine Points) + 10% VIP Bonus ) × Multiplier
* **Peak Run**: Base Score + Bonus Enemy Points + Shrine Points + 10% VIP bonus

#### Qualification

To appear on **Powerboard and Peak Run Leaderboards**, you must complete at least **20x total multiplier** (Example: 20×1x, 4×5x, 1×20x, or any other combination).

{% hint style="info" %}
_All prizes are paid in ETH to your AGW (Abstract Global Wallet) after final score validation by the Tollan Universe team_
{% endhint %}
