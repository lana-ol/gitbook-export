# Shrines & Keys System

**Collect Keys by destroying boxes and towers**.

Keys are used to **unlock Shrines**, and each Shrine requires a **specific number of Keys** to activate. You can find up to 110 Keys on the map.&#x20;

**Green dots** indicate the direction to the **closest keys** on the minimap.

**Shrine icons** are always visible on the minimap.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-01-03 at 2.04.01 AM.png" alt="" width="183"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Activating a Shrine grants **powerful buffs** and rewards you with **Points**.

#### **Cumulative Shrine Rewards**

* **1 Shrine:** 500 Points
* **2 Shrines:** 1,000 Points
* **3 Shrines:** 1,500 Points
* **4 Shrines:** 2,000 Points
* **5 Shrines:** 5,000 Points

Choosing when to unlock Shrines can significantly impact your run.
