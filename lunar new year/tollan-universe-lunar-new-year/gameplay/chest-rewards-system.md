# Chest Rewards System

{% columns %}
{% column width="58.333333333333336%" %}
* Chests have 30% chance to drop from [Bonus Enemies](bonus-enemy.md) (subject to change). All Chests drop on the map. Walk through them to collect them.
* All Chests appear in your Hub Inventory after you finish your run and must be opened before timer hits 00 or within 30 minutes after it
* Prizes include: Crowns, Abstract Stamps, Powerboard Points (see chance table for prizes below)
{% endcolumn %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
Prizes and drop rates are subject to change
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="danger" %}
* You will lose earned Chests if you quit the run
* All reward chests do not carry over to the next week
* Make sure to open them before the reset or within 30 minutes after it
{% endhint %}
