# 🐲 Lunar New Year

Lunar New Year event goes **live on February 13, 10PM UTC**, and features dragons, noodles, dumplings, Lunar Envelopes, and a limited edition **Lunar NFT Skins**.&#x20;

Survive countless waves of enemies, acquire elemental powers, earn **Abstract Stamps** while competing for a **weekly 1.5 ETH (\~3000$) prizepool** **that grows with crown purchases** — 40% of all crowns spent are added to that week’s prizepool.

{% hint style="danger" %}
IMPORTANT NOTE: Everything is subject to change. The game is in active development, and things may be adjusted, changed, removed, or updated regularly. While the core balance stays persistent during each week to avoid unfair competition, the whole economy, gameplay, monetization, pricing, etc., may be adjusted from week to week.
{% endhint %}
