# In-game Purchases

#### Affinity Reroll

* Reroll your Affinities for the current wave.&#x20;
* Can be used **up to 3 times per run**
* Cost increases with each purchase: 100 → 150 → 200 crowns (**50% off with** [**VIP Pass**](vip-pass.md))

**How it works:**

Affinity Reroll results can be 2, 3, or 4 affinities, NEVER 1.

* If you have **1 affinity** (example: Wind)
  * If the result is 2 or 3 affinities, Wind will NOT be included.
  * Wind can only appear again if you roll 4 affinities.
* If you have **2 affinities** (example: Wind + Fire)
  * If the result is **2 affinities**:
    * You get **either Wind or Fire** (only one of them), plus **Water or Energy**.
  * If the result is **3 affinities**:
    * You get **either Wind or Fire**, plus **Water and Energy**.
* If you have **3 affinities** (example: Wind + Fire + Water)
  * In **all cases**, the missing affinity (**Energy**) is **guaranteed** to appear.
  * The remaining affinities are selected randomly.

#### Skills Reroll

* Reroll the offered skills
* Can be used **up to 3 times per run**
* Cost increases with each purchase: Free → 100 → 250 crowns (**50% off with** [**VIP Pass**](vip-pass.md))
