# Week 1

### Subclasses&#x20;

Subclasses are unique character archetypes that fundamentally change how you play Tollan Universe.Each subclass comes with its own starting skill(s), unique passive abilities, and gameplay mechanics that can completely change how your run plays out. Some subclasses focus on raw power, others on survivability, and some offer unpredictable chaos.At launch, Season 5 features four playable subclasses:

* Phoenix
* Scientist
* Monk
* Oceanus

Choose wisely, your subclass will shape your entire run.Each subclass has powerful strengths and unique drawbacks, so finding the right one for your playstyle is part of the challenge!

### Encampments

The Gnoll and Orc armies have established fortified bases across the Sky Islands.Find and destroy 3 Encampments during your run:

* Destroy Arrow Towers to obtain Shrine Keys
* Destroy an Encampment to receive a special loot chest 👀

### Cannon Towers&#x20;

Cannon Towers unleash a barrage of projectiles that rain down around the player's position, creating dangerous zones that must be avoided.

### Aura Towers

Aura Towers empower every enemy on the map until they are destroyed:

🔴 Red Tower: +40% Enemy Damage \
🟢 Green Tower: +50% Enemy HP \
🔵 Blue Tower: +20% Enemy Movement Speed

Destroying an Aura Tower removes its effect and permanently grants you a bonus:

🔴 Red Tower: +3% Damage \
🟢 Green Tower: +6% HP \
🔵 Blue Tower: +4% Movement Speed

Aura Tower icons are displayed on the right side of the screen, showing which towers are active and which have already been destroyed.

Choosing which towers to destroy and when can have a significant impact on your run.

### New Equipment

Season 5 introduces new equipment slots: Amulets, Rings, Boots, Helmets, Chestplates, Staffs.

Many items are tied to specific elements, such as Arcane Amulet, Water Ring, and more.

Choosing equipment that matches your build can significantly increase your power and help you get the most out of your skills.

⚠️ New items are equipped automatically, but you can switch them in the Inventory to better match your build.

### New Enemies

#### Orc Berserker

> A brutal frontline fighter armed with a massive axe. Berserkers attack with a powerful spinning strike that damages everything around them. When wounded, they become enraged, increasing their speed and aggression, making them significantly more dangerous in the later stages of a fight.

#### Gnoll Crossbowman

> A new ranged enemy that helps defend Encampments. Crossbowmen fire arrows high into the air that land at the player's position after a short delay. Watch for the warning indicator and keep moving to avoid incoming damage.

#### Gnoll Bannerman

> The first support-type enemy in Tollan Universe. Gnoll Bannermen strengthen nearby allies with powerful auras, making them a key target during Gnoll waves. Orange Aura: increases ally Damage and Movement Speed Green Aura: significantly reduces damage taken by nearby enemies Bannermen do not attack directly, but leaving them alive can quickly turn a manageable fight into a dangerous one.
