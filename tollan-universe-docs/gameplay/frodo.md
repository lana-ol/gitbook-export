# Frodo

**Frodo** has a chance to spawn during each wave. Catch it to earn Run Points and Rank Points.

#### **Competitive Mode**

Frodo has 75% chance to drop [Chest](chests-reward-system.md) (including [Jackpot Chest](jackpot.md)).&#x20;

Frodos amount per run based on whether you have a [VIP Pass](../purchases/vip-pass.md) and/or [Hunter’s Badge](../purchases/hunters-badge.md):&#x20;

* No VIP & no HB → up to 2 Frodos per run&#x20;
* VIP or HB → up to 4&#x20;
* VIP & HB → up to 7&#x20;

Later Frodos have increased chances of dropping Jackpot Chest.

* Drops 1-500 Points and 200-700 Rank Points without VIP Pass
* Drops 400-12,500 Points and 150-1000 Rank Points with VIP Pass

#### **Casual Mode**&#x20;

Frodo has a chance to drop [Crown Jackpot Chest](jackpot.md).&#x20;

Frodos amount per run based on whether you have a Starter/Pro [Loot Pass](../purchases/loot-pass.md), [Hunter's Badge](../purchases/hunters-badge.md) and [VIP Pass](../purchases/vip-pass.md):&#x20;

* Starter & No HB → up to 2 Frodos per run&#x20;
* Starter & HB → up to 4&#x20;
* Starter & VIP → up to 4&#x20;
* Pro & No HB → up to 4&#x20;
* Starter & VIP Pass & HB → up to 7&#x20;
* Pro & HB → up to 7&#x20;

Later Frodos have increased chances of dropping Crown Jackpot Chest.

* Drops 1-500 Points and 600-1000 Rank Points with Starter Loot Pass&#x20;
* Drops 400-12,500 Points and 550-2200 Rank Points with Pro Loot Pass&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
Frodo 1-5 spawn during Wave 1-15.&#x20;

Frodo 6&7 spawn during Champion Arena.&#x20;
{% endhint %}
