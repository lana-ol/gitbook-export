# Leaderboards

All leaderboards reset _every Friday at 3PM UTC_

{% hint style="success" %}
**100% of ETH Prizepool goes to Competitive Powerboard rewards**
{% endhint %}

### Competitive Mode&#x20;

Compete across **two Leaderboards** to win ETH and Jackpot Chests:

**Powerboard:** Your cumulative score across all games, including the multipliers used.&#x20;

* Top 10 players receive fixed percentages of the Weekly Prize Pool.
* All other eligible players receive rewards proportional to their Powerboard Points.
* A minimum of 500,000 Powerboard Points is required to qualify for rewards

**Peak Run:** Your skill-based score, multiplier is not taken into account.

Rewards:

* Top 1: 2 Jackpot Chests
* Top 2: 1 Jackpot Chest
* Top 3-10: 1 Legendary Chest

{% hint style="info" %}
_Claim your ETH prize to your wallet after final score validation by the Tollan Universe team._ \
_Tollan.io -> Inventory -> Prize Claim_&#x20;
{% endhint %}

### Casual Mode

**Peak Run:** Your skill-based score

**5,200 Crowns total weekly prize pool:**

* **Top 1:** 1,000 Crowns
* **Top 2:** 500 Crowns
* **Top 3–8:** 250 Crowns each
* **Top 9–30:** 100 Crowns each

Active [**Loot Pass**](../purchases/loot-pass.md) required to participate and earn Crown rewards.

#### **Final Score Formula**

* **Competitive Powerboard:** ((Base Score + Frodo Points + Shrine Points) + 10% VIP Bonus ) × Multiplier
* **Competitive Peak Run**: Base Score + Frodo Points + Shrine Points + 10% VIP bonus
* **Casual Peak Run:** Base Score + Frodo Points + Shrine Points
