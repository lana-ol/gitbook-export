# Shrines & Shrine Keys System

**Collect Shrine Keys by destroying boxes and towers**.

Shrine Keys are used to **unlock Shrines**, and each Shrine requires a **specific number of Shrine Keys** to activate.

**Green dots** indicate the direction to the **closest Shrine Keys** on the minimap.

**Shrine icons** are always visible on the minimap.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-01-03 at 2.04.01 AM.png" alt="" width="183"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Activating a Shrine grants **powerful buffs** and rewards you with **Points**.

#### **Cumulative Shrine Rewards**

* **1 Shrine:** 1,000 Points
* **2 Shrines:** 2,000 Points
* **3 Shrines:** 3,000 Points
* **4 Shrines:** 4,000 Points
* **5 Shrines:** 5,000 Points

Choosing when to unlock Shrines can significantly impact your run.
