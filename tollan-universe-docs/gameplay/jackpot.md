# Jackpot

{% columns %}
{% column %}
* Jackpot Chest is a part of [Chest Rewards System](chests-reward-system.md)&#x20;
* Chests have 75% chance to drop from [Frodo](frodo.md) in [Competitive Mode ](competitive-mode.md)(subject to change). Jackpot chest drops on the map. Walk through it to collect it.
* Rewards include ETH (see below)
* Hit the Jackpot and claim ETH instantly in Inventory

### Jackpot Chance Booster

More crowns you spend on multipliers = Higher your Jackpot chances get

* Your Jackpot Chance Booster carries over between runs and weeks, and resets when you get Jackpot Chest
* Build up to +50% Jackpot Chance Booster
* The booster is applied after each run, so multipliers used in your current run increase your Jackpot chance starting from the next run.

\
**Jackpot Chance Booster Sliding Scale (subject to change)**&#x20;

* 1x to 40x - 0.1% per multiplier
* 41x to 50x - 0.2% per multiplier
* 51x to 60x - 0.3% per multiplier
* 61x to 70x - 0.4% per multiplier
* 71x to 80x - 0.5% per multiplier
* 81x to 90x - 0.6% per multiplier
* 91x to 100x - 0.7% per multiplier
* 101x to 200x - 0.95% per multiplier
