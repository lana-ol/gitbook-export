# Space Conquest

**Space Conquest** is a limited-time event bringing together **Tollan Universe and** [**Loot Planets**](https://x.com/LootPlanetsRH), where players can compete for free Loot Planets Space Station NFTs and guaranteed WLs for the upcoming mint.

The event runs for multiple weeks, with new rewards available each week.

### Week 1 Rewards

* **50 FREE Space Station NFTs**
* **100 Guaranteed WLs**

#### What Are Space Stations?

Space Stations are NFTs from **Loot Planets**, our upcoming game on Robinhood Chain.

* Total supply: **2,222 NFTs**
* Holders receive exclusive early access to the Loot Planets game and economy
* Mint coming soon

More info here: [https://x.com/LootPlanetsRH/status/2113693957209350216](https://x.com/LootPlanetsRH/status/2113693957209350216?a=vy-HR5AKWYFf_liyWCU\&s=20)

### How to Participate

1. Go to [tollan.io](https://tollan.io/)
2. Log in with your EVM wallet.
3. [Play Tollan Universe](how-to-play.md) to compete for Space Station NFTs and GTD WLs .

There are **4 ways to win** during Space Conquest.

### [1. Powerboard Leaderboard](../gameplay/leaderboards.md)

**Week 1 Rewards: 35 Space Station NFTs + 50 Guaranteed WLs**

Compete on the Powerboard by playing Competitive Mode and earning points.

#### How It Works

* Purchase Crowns to enter Competitive Mode.
* Select a Point Multiplier (1x–200x) before each run.
* Each 1x multiplier costs 100 Crowns.
* Your Point Multiplier determines how much your run's score contributes to the Powerboard.
* [VIP Pass](../purchases/vip-pass.md) and [Hunter's Badge](../purchases/hunters-badge.md) can help increase the points earned from your runs.

#### Rewards

* **Top 27:** Free Space Station NFT, with higher ranks receiving more NFTs.
* **Ranks 28–75:** Guaranteed WLs.

Your final position at the weekly leaderboard reset determines your rewards.

### 2. Competitive Raffle

**Weekly Rewards: 4 Space Station NFTs + 35 Guaranteed WLs**

Every Competitive Mode run can earn you raffle entries.

**1x Point Multiplier used = 1 Raffle Entry**

For example, a run using a 50x multiplier earns 50 base raffle entries.

The more entries you collect, the higher your chances of winning.

### 3. Casual Raffle

**Weekly Rewards: 1 Space Station NFT + 15 Guaranteed WLs**

Play Casual Mode with an active Loot Pass to earn raffle entries.

**1 Casual Credit spent = 1 Raffle Entry**

You can participate without competing on the Powerboard.

#### Boost Your Raffle Entries

The following passes and items increase the entries you earn in both Competitive and Casual Raffles:

| Pass / Item       | Entry Bonus |
| ----------------- | ----------- |
| VIP Pass          | +2x         |
| Hunter's Badge    | +2x         |
| Pro Loot Pass     | +2x         |
| Starter Loot Pass | +1x         |

Bonuses can be combined when you have multiple passes.

### 4. Jackpot Chests

**Weekly Rewards: 10 Space Station NFTs**

During Competitive and Casual runs, you can encounter [**Golden Frodo**](../gameplay/frodo.md), a special bonus enemy.

* Defeat Golden Frodo to collect Jackpot Chests.
* Open your Jackpot Chests in your Inventory for a chance to win a free Loot Planets Space Station NFT.

### Bonus Rewards for Abstract Tiers

Players with eligible Abstract Tiers can claim free **Space Boxes** by linking their Abstract Global Wallet (AGW) to their Tollan account.

To link your AGW, click your username in the top-left corner of the game.

#### Space Box Allocation

| **Abstract Tier** | **Free Space Boxes** |
| ----------------- | -------------------- |
| Ethereal          | 5                    |
| Obsidian          | 4                    |
| Diamond           | 3                    |
| Platinum          | 2                    |
| Gold              | 1                    |

#### What's Inside a Space Box?

Each Space Box contains:

* **1 of 9 exclusive space-themed skins**
* **10 Competitive Raffle Entries**

Space-themed skins have different rarities and drop chances.

The bonus raffle entries give you additional chances to win Space Conquest rewards.

### Weekly Schedule

Space Conquest runs for multiple weeks, with new opportunities to win each week.

**Leaderboard Reset & Raffle Drawings: Every Friday at 2:00 PM UTC**

Your weekly Powerboard position and raffle entries determine your eligibility for that week's rewards.

Space Conquest is a limited-time event and will not run indefinitely.
