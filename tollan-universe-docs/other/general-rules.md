# General Rules



* **Multi-tab play is not supported.** \
  Starting a new run in another tab will immediately cancel your current run. Unfinished runs are not recorded, and all rewards will be lost
* Bots, macros, and automation are prohibited and will result in a ban
* Offensive or vulgar in-game names may also result in a ban
* Exploiting bugs or unintended game mechanics may result in rewards being removed or your account being suspended
