# Competitive Mode

You are challenged to **survive 15 consecutive waves of enemies and Champion Arena**. Survive and defeat all enemies before time runs out or you lose.



* **Choose your subclass**&#x20;

Subclasses are unique character archetypes that change how you play Tollan Universe through special abilities, starting skills, and unique gameplay mechanics.

Each subclass offers different strengths, weaknesses, and playstyles. Choose carefully — your subclass will shape your entire run.

* **Earn Points** by defeating enemies, activating [Shrines](shrines-and-shrine-keys-system.md), catching [Frodo](frodo.md), and levelling up\*
* **Complete waves** by killing all the enemies
* **Run ends** when you run out of time or die

{% hint style="success" %}
More points you get - higher you climb on the weekly [Leaderboards](leaderboards.md).
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Multi-tab play is not supported.**\
Starting a new run will cancel your current run. Unfinished runs will not be recorded and all rewards will be lost.
{% endhint %}

#### Point Multiplier&#x20;

Select **Point Multiplier before each run** (1x–200x, 100 Crowns per 1x). Point Multiplier determines how many times your score will be recorded into the Powerboard, and it increases your Rank Points earnings based on the breakdown below:

#### Rank Points Multiplier

* 1x Rank Points for Point Multipliers 1x-4x
* 3x Rank Points for Point Multipliers 5x-14x
* 9x Rank Points for Point Multipliers 15x-39x
* 15x Rank Points for Point Multipliers 40x-79x
* 20x Rank Points for Point Multiplier 80x-200x

#### Level Cap & Bonus Points

During a run, you can level up to **Level 40**.

Once you reach the level cap, you no longer gain levels. Instead, every additional experience gain rewards you with a **random amount of Points** until the run ends.

#### Late Run Completion Window

Runs started shortly before the leaderboard timer expires must be completed within 30 minutes after the timer reaches 00:00 to count toward the leaderboard.
