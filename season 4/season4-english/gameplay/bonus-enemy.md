# Bonus Enemy

**Bonus Enemy** has a chance to spawn during each wave. Catch it to earn Powerboard Points and Abstract Stamps.

Bonus Enemy has 75% chance to drop from Chest (including Jackpot Chest) in [Seasonal Mode ](seasonal-mode.md)(subject to change). All Chests drop on the map. Walk through them to collect them.

Seasonal mode:

* Bonus Enemies amount per run based on whether you have a VIP Pass and/or Hunter’s Badge:&#x20;
  * &#x20;No VIP & no HB → up to 2 Bonus Enemies per run&#x20;
  * VIP or HB → up to 4&#x20;
  * VIP + HB → up to 7&#x20;
* Later Bonus Enemies have increased chances of dropping Jackpot Chest&#x20;
* Drops 1-500 Points and 25-300 Abstract Stamps

Practice Mode:&#x20;

* 20% chance to spawn per wave
* Drops 20-200 Abstract Stamps
* Drops Abstract Stamp Box&#x20;

{% hint style="success" %}
**Spawn chance and amount of Points and Abstract Stamps dropped can be increased** with [**VIP Pass**](../purchases/vip-pass.md) and [**Hunter’s Badge**](../purchases/hunters-badge.md)
{% endhint %}
