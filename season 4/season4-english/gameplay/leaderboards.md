# Leaderboards

All leaderboards reset _every Friday at 3PM UTC_

{% hint style="success" %}
**100% of ETH Prizepool goes to Powerboard rewards**
{% endhint %}

Compete across **two Leaderboards** to win ETH and Abstract Stamps ✳️:

* **Powerboard:** Your cumulative score across all games, including the multipliers used. Top-150 earn a share of the ETH prizepool.
* **Peak Run:** Your skill-based score, multiplier is not taken into account. Rewards:
  * Top 1: 2 Jackpot Chests
  * Top 2: 1 Jackpot Chest
  * Top 3-10: 1 Legendary Chest
  * Top 3-10: 3 raffle tickets each
  * Top 11-25: 2 raffle tickets each
  * Top 26-50: 1 raffle ticket each&#x20;
* 3 Jackpot Chests will be raffled among players in Top 3-50 of the Peak Run Leaderboard at the end of the event.\
  More raffle tickets = higher chances to win (max 1 win per player)

#### Final Score Formula

* **Powerboard:** ((Base Score + Bonus Enemy Points + Shrine Points) + 10% VIP Bonus ) × Multiplier
* **Peak Run**: Base Score + Bonus Enemy Points + Shrine Points + 10% VIP bonus

{% hint style="info" %}
_All prizes are paid in ETH to your AGW (Abstract Global Wallet) after final score validation by the Tollan Universe team_
{% endhint %}
