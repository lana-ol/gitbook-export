# Seasonal Mode

You are challenged to **survive 20 consecutive waves of enemies**. Survive and defeat all enemies before time runs out or you lose.

* **Earn Points** by defeating enemies, activating [Shrines](shrines-and-shrine-keys-system.md), catching [Bonus Enemies](bonus-enemy.md), and levelling up\*
* **Complete waves** by killing all the enemies
* **Run ends** when you run out of time or die

{% hint style="success" %}
More points you get - higher you climb on the weekly [Leaderboards](leaderboards.md).
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Multi-tab play is not supported.**\
Starting a new run will cancel your current run. Unfinished runs will not be recorded and all rewards will be lost.
{% endhint %}

#### Point Multiplier&#x20;

Select **Point Multiplier before each run** (1x–200x, 100 Crowns per 1x). Point Multiplier determines how many times your score will be recorded into the Powerboard, and it increases your Abstract Stamps earnings based on the breakdown below:

#### Abstract Stamps Multiplier

* 1x Abstract Stamps for Point Multipliers 1x-4x
* 3x Abstract Stamps for Point Multipliers 5x-14x
* 9x Abstract Stamps for Point Multipliers 15x-39x
* 15x Abstract Stamps for Point Multipliers 40x-79x
* 20x Abstract Stamps for Point Multiplier 80x-200x

#### Level Cap & Bonus Points

During a run, you can level up to **Level 40**.

Once you reach the level cap, you no longer gain levels. Instead, every additional experience gain rewards you with a **random amount of Points** until the run ends.

#### Late Run Completion Window

Runs started shortly before the leaderboard timer expires must be completed within 30 minutes after the timer reaches 00:00 to count toward the leaderboard.
