# Referral system

Refer your friends and earn ETH. The referred person is the person who created an account at [Tollan.io](https://tollan.io) using your referral link. You will find your referral link in [Referral](https://hub.tollan.io/#/referral) section.

#### Referral terms:

* Get 10% of all ETH spent by your referred players for 12 months
* Refer 5 People - get 500 crowns
* Refer 10 People - get 1500 crowns
* Refer 20 People - get 4000 crowns

{% hint style="warning" %}
Important: only referred users who make purchases will count toward your total.
{% endhint %}
